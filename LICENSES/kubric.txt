Google Research Kubric
======================

Upstream project: https://github.com/google-research/kubric
Reference revision: 61f2422c84bab75006df33c6989e0b483db3ccfe

Kubric's deterministic segmentation rules and generated outputs are used as
test-oracle evidence for SceneIO's semantic, instance, and panoptic label-map
contracts. Kubric and Blender are not runtime or wheel dependencies, and no
Kubric source code is copied into SceneIO.

Copyright ownership and attribution remain with the upstream Kubric
contributors at the pinned revision; SceneIO does not invent or replace an
upstream copyright notice.
